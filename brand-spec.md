## Supporting color values

- Deep blue: `#062B49` / `oklch(28.2% 0.068 248.0)`
- Navy: `#0A3A5E` / `oklch(33.8% 0.080 246.6)`
- Tech cyan: `#35BDF2` / `oklch(75.0% 0.135 229.5)`
- Energetic orange: `#FF7A1A` / `oklch(72.4% 0.187 48.9)`
- Ice white: `#F7FBFF` / `oklch(98.6% 0.007 247.9)`
- Elegant gray: `#6B7280` / `oklch(55.1% 0.023 264.4)`
- Soft black: `#111827` / `oklch(21.0% 0.032 264.7)`

## Typography

- Display: `'Sora', 'Manrope', 'Space Grotesk', system-ui, sans-serif`
- Body: `'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', system-ui, sans-serif`
- Mono: `'JetBrains Mono', 'IBM Plex Mono', ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, monospace`

## Layout posture

- Premium Liquid Glass Corporate Tech: white space, translucent cards, soft blur, subtle depth.
- Use deep navy for trust and structural contrast; use cyan as the main technology accent.
- Reserve orange for high-intent CTAs and a few growth markers only.
- Rounded glass panels can use 22-32px radii, 1px translucent borders, and restrained shadows.
- Product visuals should look like business operations dashboards, not developer portfolio screenshots.
